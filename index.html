Selected work

Things I've built and researched

Dust detection & cleaning2024 – 2025
BRAC University
Technical lead

A system that spots dust and cleans it on its own

I led the build of a real-time system on a Raspberry Pi. It uses image segmentation to find dust, then triggers the cleaning hardware through GPIO. Alerts go to a mobile app, and I trained my teammates on both the hardware and the software.

  • AI
  • Embedded
  • Raspberry Pi
  • Python
  • Image segmentation
Smart grid researchJan – Jun 2024
Smart Grid and Energy Informatics Lab
Undergraduate researcher

Catching abnormal behaviour in the power grid

I designed a load monitoring and anomaly detection system using CNN-LSTM and Informer models. It combines electrical, environmental and economic data to predict grid behaviour in real time. I also studied how the models make decisions, so the results could support grid optimisation.

  • Machine learning
  • Energy
  • CNN-LSTM
  • Informer
  • Time-series prediction

About me

Engineer, mentor, still curious

I grew up in Bangladesh and studied Electrical and Electronic Engineering at BRAC University in Dhaka, graduating in October 2025. The more I learned, the more I wanted to work where power, hardware and intelligence meet: machines that look after themselves, and grids that flag their own faults.

I enjoy teaching as much as building. I worked as an undergraduate teaching assistant for two years, mentored project teams at the IEEE Student Branch, and visited rural schools to talk about engineering.
